Design decisions

Up: SoftPack

The design cards on the board each asked one question and listed the options. This section records the answer that is now in the code, why it was chosen, and what it changed. The design document (docs/design-references.md) stays the specification; a decision here says how an open point in it was settled.

The rule behind every choice was the same: prefer the option that makes a run easier to reproduce and to explain, even when it costs a little speed. A packing is a scientific result, so a setting must mean one thing, a file must say how it was made, and a run that goes wrong must leave its work where you can look at it.
