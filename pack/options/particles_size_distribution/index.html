particles_size_distribution

Up: Available options

particles_size_distribution chooses the law that the particle diameters are drawn from. The default is a 50:50 mixture of two diameters in the ratio 1.4, the mixture that prevents crystallization in 2D jamming studies. Each choice fixes the length unit through its own defaults: Bidisperse() makes the small diameter 1, UniformPolydisperse() makes the mean diameter 1, and Monodisperse() makes its one diameter 1. Every output stores each particle's diameter.

The call with the default written out

pack(                                           # run the packing
    particles_size_distribution = Bidisperse()  # two-diameter mixture (default)
)                                               # end of pack call
  • pack(...) — runs the packing.
  • particles_size_distribution = Bidisperse() (default) — a 50:50 mixture of two diameters in the ratio 1.4.
    • For details, see Bidisperse().
