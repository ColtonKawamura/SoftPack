CundallStrack()

Up: friction

A linear tangential spring with a Coulomb cap (design §13). The tangential force is

\[f_t = -k_t \xi,\]

with $\xi$ the tangential displacement stored since the contact was made, and the force is clipped at $\mu f_n$: once $|f_t|$ reaches the cap the contact is sliding, the stored displacement stops growing in the direction of the cap, and the flag is reported in the contact table.

The tangential stiffness is a fixed fraction of the normal one, $k_t = r\,k_n$. The default, stiffness_ratio = :matched, gives $r = 1/3$ in 2D and $r = 2/7$ in 3D. Those are the values that make the tangential contact time equal the normal one when the tangential effective mass includes the particle's rotation, which is the mass the tangential spring actually moves; with them, the normal and tangential springs are equally stiff in time and neither sets a shorter time step than the other.

The tangential force exerts a torque on each particle, $\tau = -R \times f_t$, so rotation and spin are part of the dynamics. Friction applies during the minimization and during damped dynamics alike.

Options

OptionDefaultMeaning
friction_coefficient0.5$\mu$ in the Coulomb cap
stiffness_ratio:matched$k_t/k_n$; :matched is $1/3$ in 2D and $2/7$ in 3D, or give a number

Example

pack(friction = CundallStrack(friction_coefficient = 0.3))
