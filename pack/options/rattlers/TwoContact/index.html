TwoContact()

Up: rattlers

Remove every particle with two or fewer contacts [Ishima2023], repeating the removal until the network stops changing. It is the strict version of ContactCount(): even a frictional packing, where two contacts can hold a particle, loses its two-contact particles.

It has no cone_test: a particle with two contacts is removed whether or not its friction cones could hold it, so the test would never be reached.

This is the rule to use when the backbone should have at least three contacts per particle at every friction coefficient, for example when comparing with a measurement that defines the contact network that way.

Options

None.

Example

using SoftPack

packing = pack(
    rattlers = TwoContact(),
    particles_count = 256,
    seed = 1,
)
count(packing.rattlers)
