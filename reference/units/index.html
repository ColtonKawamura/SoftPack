Units and length unit

Up: SoftPack

SoftPack works in reduced units: the numbers in a run are pure numbers, and a physical unit is attached only at the end, when a result is compared with an experiment.

QuantityReferenceDefault
length$d_\text{ref}$, the small diameter of the mixture1
mass$m_\text{ref}$: the EqualMass mass, or the mean mass with Density1
stiffness$k$: the stiffness of the contact law1
time$\sqrt{m_\text{ref}/k}$1
pressure$k/d_\text{ref}^{D-1}$1
energy$k\,d_\text{ref}^D$1

The packing fraction and the coordination number are dimensionless and do not depend on the units at all, which is why they are the numbers a run reports first.

Changing the length unit

A run with every diameter multiplied by $\lambda$ and the same $k$ and $m$ is the same packing: $\phi$ and $z$ do not change, and only the time unit changes, by $\sqrt{\lambda^3}$ for a mass that follows the volume or not at all for an equal mass. That invariance is what lets a packing made in reduced units be quoted at any physical size.

For a physical mapping, choose three numbers and derive the rest: for disks of 1 mm with $k = 100\ \text{N/m}$ and $m = 1\ \text{g}$, the time unit is $\sqrt{10^{-3}/100}\ \text{s} \approx 3\ \text{ms}$ and the pressure unit is $k/d = 10^5\ \text{Pa}$.
