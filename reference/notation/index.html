Notation

Up: SoftPack

The symbol tables, with units per contact law. They are the same as the wiki's Notation page; the option pages define their own symbols where they first appear.

Every symbol used in the design doc and the wiki, with its units in SoftPack's reduced units (reference diameter $d_\text{ref} = 1$, contact stiffness $k = 1$, reference mass $m_\text{ref} = 1$; see §3, §2 and §4). The Units columns write $d$ and $m$ for one $d_\text{ref}$ and one $m_\text{ref}$. Each page also defines its symbols where they first appear.

The units depend on the contact law through the exponent $\alpha$ of $U(\delta) = (k/\alpha)\,\delta^{\alpha}$ ($\alpha = 2$ for Hooke, $5/2$ for Hertz; §2). The columns give the general form and then the Hookean value; the Hertzian one follows by setting $\alpha = 5/2$:

QuantityGeneralHooke ($\alpha = 2$)Hertz ($\alpha = 5/2$)
force$k\,d^{\alpha-1}$$k\,d$$k\,d^{3/2}$
energy$k\,d^{\alpha}$$k\,d^2$$k\,d^{5/2}$
pressure, stress$k\,d^{\alpha-D}$$k\,d^{2-D}$$k\,d^{5/2-D}$
time$\sqrt{m\,d^{2-\alpha}/k}$$\sqrt{m/k}$$\sqrt{m\,d^{-1/2}/k}$

At the default $k = d = 1$ every column reads 1, which is why a run quotes numbers without a unit and a comparison between the two laws can be made at the same target pressure. Away from it the same target number means a different overlap, and so different physics, under the two laws. SoftPack note.

Conventions

  • $\gamma$ is a dashpot coefficient, force per velocity. It is never a rate.
  • Rates (inverse time) are $r$ for contact dashpots, with $\gamma = m_\text{eff}\,r$, and $a$ for Stokes drag.
  • Rayleigh damping is written $\boldsymbol\Gamma = a\mathbf M + b\mathbf K$: $a$ is the mass-proportional constant (Stokes drag), $b$ the stiffness-proportional one.
  • $c$ and $C$ mean wave speed only, never damping. SoftPack does not use them.
  • Quotes keep the source's own symbols, even where they clash with these. The text around the quote says what the source's symbol is in SoftPack's notation, for example Silbert et al.'s rate $\gamma_n$, which is our $r_n$.

Symbols with two meanings

These clash between sources. Each page says which meaning it uses.

SymbolMeanings
$\alpha$contact-law exponent; FIRE mixing parameter (§14); a tensor index in $\sigma_{\alpha\beta}$
$\sigma$stress tensor $\sigma_{\alpha\beta}$; contact distance $\sigma_{ij}$ in [OHern2003]'s potential
$d$one $d_\text{ref}$ in the Units columns; the dimension in [OHern2003]'s quotes (SoftPack writes $D$)
$P$pressure. FIRE's power $\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v$ is $W$ here; the FIRE papers and LAMMPS call it $P$ (§14)
$\beta$mode decay rate $\beta_\ell$; a tensor index in $\sigma_{\alpha\beta}$
$\boldsymbol\omega$particle spin (bold, a vector); $\omega$ (plain) an angular frequency
$\gamma$SoftPack: coefficient. LAMMPS, [Silbert2001] and [Santos2024]: a rate (our $r$)
